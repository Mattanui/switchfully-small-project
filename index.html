Hi, I'm Andreas Pettiaux

Psychologist turned Business Analyst & Web Developer

With a background as a psychologist and psycho-educational counselor, I've always been driven by a deep interest in human behavior and how people interact with their environment. My passion for digital innovation in the educational and psychosocial sectors naturally guided me toward the role of Business Analyst, where I used my analytical skills to design human-centric technological solutions.

My experience working with diverse groups (in schools, institutions, and prisons) is built on a strong foundation in forensic psychology, complemented by my skills in web development. This dual expertise is my greatest asset in deeply understanding business needs and translating them into well-structured digital solutions.

I'm a naturally curious and solution-oriented person who values teamwork, active listening, and adaptability in project management. Lately, as a Business Analyst at Civadis, I acted as a bridge between stakeholders and technical teams, modeling processes to ensure the quality and relevance of the software solutions we build.
